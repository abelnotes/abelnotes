Handwriting search reads lines with the help of a word list
(assets/models/handwriting_lexicon.tsv): Italian and English words with how
often they occur.

The word frequencies are adapted from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
(https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, 2018 Italian and English lists,
built from OpenSubtitles), licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: words lower-cased with accents removed, kept only when made of the
letters a-z, the most frequent ones of both languages merged with some
technical words, and turned into log-probabilities. This adapted list is
shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
